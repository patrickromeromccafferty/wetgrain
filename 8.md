## 8 <br><br>

Mourning turns the page. A dazed lily twirls <br>
on its stem. Death, irredeemable number, <br>
scatters bone. White musk curls in silk whorls. <br>
My heart is penniless. The touch of skin<br>
is the touch of stone. A crisp hydrangea <br>
consoles a window. Induplicable<br>
instants inure us to loss. My heart petrifies. <br>
The dark wall is a corridor, a toreador<br>
to an absent bull, charging nothing.<br>
Is the force too great to wield? Words condense, <br>
heard, to sense. The canon unravels.<br>
A lone voice reels. I want the bone healed.<br>
O valediction, all the clocks, rage, rage.<br>
Time comes true. Mourning turns the page.<br>

<br><br><br>

*Sylee Gore*
