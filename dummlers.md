## Dummler’s <br><br>

Whenever I’m asked why I’m not doing <br>
something that the rest are all doing<br>
<br>
I think of Dummler’s, the long gone <br>
pork butchers on Gateshead high street<br>
<br>
which was pelted with bricks and stones <br>
at the outbreak of World War One<br>
<br>
by good patriotic folk who thought <br>
the Dummler family were all Huns.<br>
<br>
They turned out to be Belgian, <br>
Poor little Belgium and all that.<br>
<br>
My great grandmother liked to go <br>
there for saveloy butties in gravy.<br>
<br>
It was the detail of her account <br>
that put her at the crime scene too.<br>
<br><br>
*Richie McCaffery*
