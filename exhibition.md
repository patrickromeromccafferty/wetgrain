### Exhibition <br>
*Efter ‘Behind the Lines’* <br>
<br>
I spok aboot this wae Simon, efterwards. <br>
The wey wan object<br>
kin change a whole exhibition.<br>
Withoot hid those Orkney artists’ draaeens <br>
widda been more o whit ye might expect: <br>
a fisherman in a raffle o nets,<br>
creels up the pierheid waal, botts in the basin;<br>
a fermer gannan oot the back o his tractor<br>
at the dreel beneath; dry stane dykes bright <br>
wae lichen and the big skies, af coorse.<br>
And a group o young men,<br>
in-aboot the links o the fourth barrier, <br>
perched oan flat stones roond a peedie fire; <br>
twa couples oan the cathedral steps, <br>
laughan intae thir hands.<br>
<br>
Hid wis artworks more<br>
populated than whit wir winked wae—<br>
that wis a differ right there.<br>
Bit the AK-47 transformed the draaeens <br>
completely—jarred so much<br>
wae whit shoulda been—<br>
hitched across backs, hung fae the shoolder, <br>
propped up against oot-buildeens and legs. <br>
Hid waarped the simple act o mark-makeen— <br>
became centre o gravity<br>
and hids negation—double-faced emblem<br>
o guerrilla waarfare and the mercenary<br>
<br>
like a dark splinter at thir heels;<br>
young weeman, suppan oan mugs<br>
o home-made soup, dunkan hunks o loff. <br>
And whit else wis at thir feet?<br>
A scafoldeen knuckle? A drain pipe?<br>
If this wis phottos<br>
hid widda been different.<br>
Wir used wae photography’s unsettlement. <br>
Bit this wis pastel and graphite<br>
in a peedie touristy gallery up St Olaf ’s closs, <br>
fudge fur sale oan the coonter,<br>
an Orkney chair huggan hidseal bae the fire. <br>
Years Ago, hid whispered.<br>
<br>
<br>
*Kevin Cormack*
