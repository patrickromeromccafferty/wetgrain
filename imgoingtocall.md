## i’m going to name this poem burn out is for pussys <br>
<br>
i live across from the cemetery<br>
so i see a lot of folk dressed in Sunday<br>
<br>
best sat on camping chairs saying names<br>
that haven’t echoed for ages &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Sara<br>
<br>
& i can’t decide whether it’s more<br>
worthy to be in *duty* or in *service* to our people<br>
<br>
anyway she prays & i do the same take another<br>
media interview about the latest burglary charges against us<br>
<br>
we complain we smoke too much but it ain’t a problem<br>
in fact *it’s the only time i take a deep breathe all day* Taz says<br>
<br>
& me & Cooke nod & *innit yeah same*<br>
<br>
i try herding my angels into one room<br>
at night i’m a little bit sheepdog<br>
<br>
surrendering &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the fan rotates at our feet<br>
a sunflower oblivious to which way she’s facing<br><br><br>

*lisa minerva luxx*
