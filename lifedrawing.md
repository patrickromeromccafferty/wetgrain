### Life Drawing I <br>
<br>
<br>
Do not assume that you know what an ear looks like. <br>
<br>
I heard an astronaut on the radio today saying <br>
that while he isn’t afraid of heights, he is afraid <br>
of gravity; and imagine my surprise <br>
<br>
when I realised <br>
that there is no word for ‘kindness’ in German. <br>
<br>
If you didn’t know about wind, <br>
you would think that the trees were dancing. <br>
<br><br>

*Iona Lee*
