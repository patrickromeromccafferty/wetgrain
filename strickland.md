### A Move Of Even A Single Second Should Be Taken As An Indication <br>
<br>

A hard rain is falling again, though not today. <br>
A harder rain than Dylan sang, though not today. <br>
<br>
A day so piercingly beautiful, you’ll mistake it <br>
For diamond or peacetime, though not today. <br>
<br>
Night is a shower of bullets, though not tonight. <br>
Black radioactive fallout, though not tonight. <br>
<br>
Silently, we hold each other instead of our dying <br>
Phones after we make love, though not tonight. <br>
<br>
We run from the living room to find our daughter <br>
Where others find moonlight, though not now. <br>

<br>
<br>

*Taylor Strickland*
