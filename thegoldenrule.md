## The Golden Rule <br><br>

Mu dheireadh thall tha mi air toiseachdainn air an dàn fad’ agam mu dheidhinn fàilte, <br>
aoigheachd, lagh naomh nan Gàidheal, a chuireadh dleastanas ort fasgadh <br>
a thoirt do duine sam bith, caraid no nàmhaid, mar gum b’e dluth-dàimheach a bh’unnta<br>
agus a dhearbhas gur iad nan Caimbeulaich ann an Gleann Comhainn<br>
an gràisg as miosa a bh’ann riamh, dàn a bha mi an dòchas a dhèanadh fèill<br>
<br>
air gach inimrich a dh’Alba, agus feum againn orra (aig a’ char as lugha)<br>
gus comhar a thoirt seachad nar taighean-cùraim, nar taighean-osta, ospadalan,<br>
B&Ban, gun cus feart a thoirt ris an eachdraidh fhada againn de ghràin-chinneadh<br>
agus colonachd – ach tha mi a-nis gam lorg ann an staing<br>
leis gur e seo seachdainn anns an robh Dòmhnall Iain Trump a’ cèilidh<br>
<br>
air an tìr seo, tìr a tha e gu math toilichte aithneachadh airson abhdharan<br>
prothaid ’s poileataigs, agus tha mi a-nis a’ meòrachadh<br>
air crìochan ‘fàilte’ agus le sin mu dheidhinn an ‘gouden rule’<br>
anns an sgeulachd aig Samuel Scrape, Cameronian à Penpunt,<br>
anns na *Private Memoirs and Confessions* aig James Hogg,<br>
<br>
riaghailt òr air an tug Lucky Shaw iomradh dha, gun fhuasgladh a thoirt dhuinn<br>
dè dìreach tha i a’ ciallachadh – ‘gach uile ni bu mhiann leibh<br>
daoine a dheanamh dhuibhse, deanaibhse a leithid dhoibhsan mar an ceudna’<br>
neo rudeigin mar sin, ’s dòcha – cuir earbsa nad shùilean agus chan e briathran faoin<br>
mar ‘choltas’, gu h-àraidh nuair a tha thu a’ faicinn crodhan fo chasag,<br>
<br>
agus le sin a-rithist a’ smaoineachadh air cuairt nan dealbh anns a qhuiz san Golden Rule,<br>
an ‘local’ agam ma tha fear ann, leis an fhàilte neònach a bhiodh ri fhaighinn sa leithid,<br>
far a bheil feum agad aithneachadh an còmhnaidh an actair agus chan e an caracatar.<br>
Cò tha nochdadh ann an riochd aoigh neo òstair? Cò a chluich Dracula ann an *Gremlins 2*?<br>

## The Golden Rule<br><br>

Having finally started work on my long poem about welcome,<br>
about hospitality, that great inviolable rule of the Gàidheal<br>
which would have you shelter anyone, friend or foe,<br>
beneath your roof as if they were your dearest kin,<br>
and which casts the Campbells at Glencoe as the worst of all beings,<br>
<br>
a poem I hoped would celebrate all immigrants to Scotland,<br>
who we need (at the very least) to provide succour<br>
in our care homes, hospitals, hotels and butt and bens,<br>
while also being generous about our only slowly unfurled<br>
histories of racism and colonial exploitation – I found myself stuck,<br>
<br>
having foolishly begun in a week that Dòmhnall Iain Trump was visiting<br>
if not his homeland then at least a land he would happily claim as his own<br>
for financial and political gain, and took to wondering<br>
about the limits of welcome, and about the ‘gouden rule’<br>
in the story told by Samuel Scrape, the Cameronian from Penpunt<br>
<br>
in James Hogg’s *Private Memoirs and Confessions*, a rule that is mentioned<br>
but never explained by Lucky Shaw as a way of knowing the devil:<br>
‘do unto others as you would have done to you’, I suppose<br>
we’re meant to take it, or ‘trust your eyes’ not malleable words<br>
like ‘likeness’, especially when you see a cloven hoof under a minister’s smock,<br>
<br>
and with that again wondering about the picture round in the quiz in The Golden Rule<br>
(my local, if a local I have one, with the peculiar sort of welcome that entails)<br>
in which you have to always identify the actor and not the character played.<br>
Who goes there in the role of guest and host? Which jobbing actor played Dracula<br>
in *Gremlins 2*? Who was Lord Summerisle in the *Wicker Man*? How did we end up here?<br>
<br><br>
*Peter Mackay*
