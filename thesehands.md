## These Hands <br><br>

these hands,<br>
more often than not,<br>
<br>
are the sticks my brother would bring home <br>
from nursery, pretending <br>
<br>
they were guns,<br>
but on my best days<br>
<br>
these hands are able <br>
to hold cloudberries<br>
<br>
without having them <br>
blow away,<br>
<br>
and this is how men <br>
love each other–<br>
<br>
in my daydreams <br>
men love<br>
<br>
like the fairy ring <br>
in the backyard of my childhood home <br>
<br>
which, no matter the time <br>
of year, or how many times<br>
<br>
my dad mows the lawn,<br>
is always unflinchingly there<br>
<br>
holding us<br>
together, arm in arm.<br>
<br><br>
*Carl Alexandersson*
