## the word *hope* has no weight, i prefer to say *faith* <br>
<br>
haven’t heard a song sung<br>
down the tannoy of the london<br>
underground for a while but 48<br>
<br>
new guerrilla billboards went up<br>
as an oud played over the techno we<br>
emptied our banks started writing again<br>
<br>
what would we be doing if Black Britain<br>
hadn’t shown us cultural resistance<br>
perhaps still installing locks on our doors<br>
<br>
trusting a district judge to know<br>
how it feels to live like this<br>
my parents started going to the movies again<br>
<br>
by now everyone i love has been arrested<br>
i keep hiding flags in the pockets of my long coat<br>
every time i think it’s over –<br>
<br>
a widowed man with the tremors<br>
appears on the bridge<br>
blowing dust off the chess board<br>
straightening each rook and elephant<br><br><br><br>

*lisa minerva luxx*
